# OpenAI Plugin Submission

## Listing

- Submission type: Skills only
- Plugin name: Natural Science Learning Design
- Category: Education
- Short description: Review Natural Science learning designs through CBCI, KUD, Structure of Knowledge, and IBL while preserving teacher agency.
- Long description: Natural Science Learning Design gives Vietnamese lower-secondary science teachers evidence-based feedback on lesson and unit designs. It reviews generalizations, KUD goals, guiding questions, Structure of Knowledge, learning experiences, assessment alignment, student-centred organization, and differentiation. Every full review follows a four-part format and offers a feasible alternative learning sequence as a teacher-reviewed option. It does not write a complete lesson plan or replace professional judgment.
