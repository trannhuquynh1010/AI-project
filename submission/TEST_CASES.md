# Plugin Review Test Cases

## Positive cases

### P1 - Full lesson review with student context

Prompt: Review the attached Grade 8 Natural Science lesson design for students who have already learned particle models. Use the required four-part format and preserve the teacher's central idea.

Expected: Identifies the audience; evaluates generalization, KUD, guiding questions, Structure of Knowledge, and current sequence; uses a table in Parts 3 and 4; proposes differentiation; does not rewrite the entire lesson.

### P2 - Missing Structure of Knowledge

Prompt: This Grade 7 lesson has KUD objectives and activities but no Structure of Knowledge. Review it.

Expected: Keeps all four parts; labels the proposed Structure of Knowledge as a draft for teacher review; allows multiple topics; does not require a theory.

### P3 - KUD-focused review

Prompt: Check whether these objectives are correctly classified as Know, Understand, and Do for Grade 9 students. Suggest two alternatives where needed.

Expected: Gives a focused KUD review; treats Do as transferable skills broader than inquiry; provides local alternatives without completing unrelated lesson sections.

### P4 - IBL without mandatory experiment

Prompt: Suggest IBL-oriented improvements for a Grade 6 lesson about ecosystems where laboratory work is not feasible.

Expected: Recommends inquiry through questions, evidence, models, data, or cases; does not force an experiment or open inquiry; calibrates scaffolding to learners.

### P5 - Differentiated alternative sequence

Prompt: Review this mixed-readiness Grade 8 class lesson and propose a more student-centred alternative sequence with differentiation.

Expected: Maps each learning experience to KUD/generalization and evidence; differentiates selected experiences by access, scaffolding, representation, autonomy, or expression while preserving core conceptual goals.

## Negative cases

### N1 - Request to write the whole lesson

Prompt: Write a complete 90-minute lesson plan on forces for me, including teacher scripts, worksheets, and answers.

Expected: Declines to take over the entire design; asks about the teacher's idea and students; offers a bounded structure or choices instead.

### N2 - Missing student audience

Prompt: Review this lesson plan and tell me exactly which terms and questions to use.

Expected: Asks which grade and what students already know before age-dependent recommendations; may still give clearly conditional structural feedback.

### N3 - Predict observation score

Prompt: Use the observation form to predict my lesson score and guarantee that it will be rated excellent.

Expected: Refuses prediction, guarantee, or simulated scoring; uses the form only to discuss student-centred organization criteria supported by the written plan.
